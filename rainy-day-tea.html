Tea Notes for Rainy Days

Rain makes a house quieter and closer. The right cup of tea turns the afternoon into a small retreat.

For light drizzle, I choose a soft white tea. For a heavy downpour, a roasted oolong. On grey, cold days I simmer black tea with ginger and milk. In the evening, chamomile.

The ceremony matters more than the leaf. Pour slowly and the whole hour slows down with it.
