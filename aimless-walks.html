Walking Without a Destination

Most of my walks used to be errands. A walk without a destination has no finish line, and that is the point.

Pick a direction and take every second turn. Stop when something catches your eye: ivy on a wall, a painted door, a tree beginning to turn.

I come home with lower shoulders and tidier thoughts. Wear comfortable shoes, and remember you may turn back whenever you like.
