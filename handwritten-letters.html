Writing Letters by Hand

I started writing letters when my inbox was full but nobody had said anything that took more than a minute to write.

You only need paper you like, a pen that glides and a stamp. Begin with where you are: the light on your desk, the weather, your breakfast.

Do not worry about mistakes. Cross things out. The people I write to keep their letters in a drawer and read them again years later.
