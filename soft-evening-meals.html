Soft Meals for Tired Evenings

Some evenings cooking feels like a mountain. These meals live between a big effort and ordering in.

Try one-pot rice: soften an onion in butter, add rice and stock, then fold in spinach and lemon at the end. Or roast tomatoes, garlic and onion, blend them with stock and eat with toast.

Keep rice, stock, canned tomatoes, eggs and lemons at home. Dinner does not have to be impressive to be an act of care.
