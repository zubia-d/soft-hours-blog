A Small Garden on a Windowsill

I live on the third floor with one sunny window. I thought that meant no garden, until a friend gave me a cutting of mint in a jam jar.

Herbs are the kindest place to start. Mint and chives forgive almost everything. Basil likes warmth. Water when the top of the soil feels dry.

Leaves will yellow sometimes. Treat it as a conversation, not a failure. The plant is telling you what it needs.
